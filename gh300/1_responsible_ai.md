# Responsible AI:
- developing, assessing, and deploying AI systems in safe, trustworthy and ethical way

- Six principles of Responsible AI:
    - Fairness - treat people fairly
    - Reliability and safety
    - Privacy and Security - secure, respect privacy
    - Inclusiveness - empower everyone
    - Transparency - should be understandable
    - Accountability - People should be accountable for AI systems

- Fairness:
    - review training data
    - testing models with balanced demographic samples
    - adversarial debiasing
    - monitoring model performance across user segments
    - controls to override unfair model scores

- Reliability and safety:
    - function as designed, respond safely to unexpected situations, resist harmful manipulations

- Privacy and Security:
    - protect user privacy and trust
        - How:
            - Getting user permission prior to data collection
            - Collecting only data needed for AI to work
            - Anonymise sensitive data:
                - pseudonymization
                - aggregation
            - encrypt sensitive info while in transit and when storing
                - hardware security modules
                - secure vaults
                - envelope encryption
                - control who can access keys

- Inclusiveness
    - AI systems work well for diverse users and groups
    - are accessible
    - available worldwide
    - all users to benefit equally from AI

    - examples:
        - facial recognition that works across skin tones
        - interfaces that support screen readers for visually impaired
        - language translation that supports small regional dialects
        - teams that seek diverse perspectives when designing systems

- Transparency
    - Explain how their systems operate clearly through clear validation framework
    - Justify design choices behind AI systems
    - Be honest about capabilities and limitations
    - Enable auditability

- Accountabiity:
    - AI systems must be accountable to people, and companies deploying AI systems must take responsibility for their operation.
